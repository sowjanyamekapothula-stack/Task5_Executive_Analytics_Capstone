# EdVyro Task 4 – Executive Analytics Capstone

## Customer Churn Analysis

### Project Overview
The objective of this project is to analyze customer churn patterns, identify important business insights, and provide recommendations to improve customer retention.

### Dataset Information
- Dataset: Customer Churn Sample
- Total Customers: 15
- Overall Churn Rate: 46.67%
- Average Customer Tenure: 18.8 Months
- Average Monthly Charges: 84.66

### Five Key Business Insights

1. Overall customer churn rate is 46.67%.
2. Month-to-month contract customers showed 100% churn.
3. One-year and two-year contract customers showed 0% churn.
4. Customers with three or more support tickets showed 100% churn.
5. Average customer tenure is 18.8 months.

### Business Recommendations

- Improve customer support services.
- Investigate repeated customer complaints.
- Collect feedback from customers who leave.
- Review suitable contract options for customers.
- Monitor customer churn regularly.

### Limitations

This project uses a small sample dataset containing only 15 customers. Therefore, the findings are exploratory and cannot be generalized to all customers. The observed patterns do not prove cause and effect.

This project demonstrates how data analytics can help businesses understand customer churn patterns and make better decisions to improve customer retention.
